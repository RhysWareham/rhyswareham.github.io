01 · Featured · ShadowSlot Studios

Royal Rift

Coming soon

The first game from my own studio: a pixel-art slot I'm building end to end. I wrote the maths model and simulations in Python, the client in TypeScript with Svelte 5 and PixiJS 8, and drew the pixel art in Aseprite. I also learnt Spine for this game to animate the symbols, wins and free spins.

Land three scatters and the sky shatters into night, a rift tears open above the castle, and the free spins begin.

  • 5×4 reels · 40 lines
  • ×2–×10 multiplier wilds
  • Collector wild
  • 8 / 11 / 15 free spins
  • Bonus buy
  • 5,000× max win

    03 · About

    A bit about me

    I founded ShadowSlot Studios, where I make slot games from start to finish: the maths, the game client, the art and the testing. Until November 2026 I was Lead Game Developer at Massive Studios, turning designs into HTML5 slot games: gameplay features, reel mechanics, UI, and the architecture that keeps it all maintainable in a fast-moving production. Before that I led development on Shamrock Saints and Power Paws at Push Gaming.

    Away from work I build in Unity and C#. I love crafting gameplay mechanics and enemy AI for desktop and mobile, and lately I've been exploring procedural animation for more dynamic enemies. That interest started with Kurat, a TIGA-nominated university game where I built the physics-based combat and the boss AI.

    I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Games Programming from the University of Gloucestershire. Right now I'm finishing Royal Rift and making a cosy cafe game with my partner.
